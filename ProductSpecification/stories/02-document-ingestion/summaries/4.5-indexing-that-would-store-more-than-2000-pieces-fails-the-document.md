## green-usecase (2026-11-09)

**Surprise:** Calling `ExtractedText.pieces()` before the token branch of `over_piece_cap` on `many_pieces.pdf` (~1M tokens) hangs long enough that the document never reaches failed or ready.
**Why:** Piece splitting walks the full token stream; the cheap token-cap check was only reached after that walk.
**Impact:** `IndexDocuments` must run `over_piece_cap(0, text)` before `pieces()`; do not invoke `pieces()` on uncapped extracted text.

## design (2026-11-09)

**Decision:** Fail over-cap PDFs with `too many pieces` via a token-cap check before splitting; keep the piece-count check after `pieces()` for the non-token path.
**Why:** The fixture exceeds `PIECE_CAP * TOKENS_PER_PIECE` without needing a completed split.
**Where applied:** `backend/usecase/document/index_documents.py`, `indexing_budget.over_piece_cap`
