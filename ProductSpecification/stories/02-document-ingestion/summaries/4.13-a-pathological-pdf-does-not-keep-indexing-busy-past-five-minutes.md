## design (2026-11-09)

**Decision:** Keep the five-minute `IndexingBudget` deadline from 4.6; run `PyPdfReader` parse in `asyncio.to_thread` so a costly real read can be abandoned when the deadline cancels the await; after fail+cancel, allowing the reader later must not change status or write pieces.
**Why:** hz-06 needs a pathological/costly read that cannot keep indexing busy past the ceiling; sync pymupdf on the event loop would block the deadline poll.
**Where applied:** `backend/adapters/pdfreader/pdf_reader.py`; acceptance uses a padded real lecture (synthetic `_pdf_of_size` fails pymupdf) + blocked read + clock advance + `_until_terminal`.
