## design (2026-11-09)

**Decision:** `VirusCheck.clear(content)` port on `SubmitDocument` (before idempotency/rates/store); acceptance uses `ProbeVirusCheck`; production uses `ClamavVirusCheck` (zINSTREAM over TCP). Clears → continue; finds virus / timeout / unreadable → `InvalidDocument("virus_detected")` and no row.
**Why:** Spec refuses unclean uploads at once; settings already required `CLAMAV_HOST` without a caller.
**Where applied:** `backend/usecase/document/submit_document.py`, `adapters/virus/clamav.py`, acceptance `ProbeVirusCheck`.
