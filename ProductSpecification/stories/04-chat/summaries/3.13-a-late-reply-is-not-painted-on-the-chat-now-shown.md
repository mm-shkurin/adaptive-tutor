**Quirk:** Acceptance `SendMessage` uses a frozen clock with a 50ms latency budget, so `asyncio.wait_for` times out on wall clock while a tutor hold is open.
**Where:** `acceptance/support/acceptance_server.py`, `/acceptance/chat/latency`
**Implication:** Selenium hold scenarios must stretch latency (e.g. 30s) before arming the hold, or the late reply never stores a tutor line.
